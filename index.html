FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Polybot?

A hosted platform that turns a strategy written in plain English into a trading bot on Kalshi and Polymarket, runs it next to the exchange with hard risk limits, and logs every order with its latency. It is the stack we built and ran with our own money from September to October 2026.

Do you hold my funds?

No. On Kalshi you give an API key scoped to trading; on Polymarket you connect your own wallet. We place and cancel orders in your name. You can revoke access at any time.

Will the bot make money?

That depends on your rule, and Polybot is built to find out fast: backtest on real order books in minutes, paper-trade on live prices for free, then go live with hard limits. We ran 170 strategies through this exact loop ourselves. Execution, speed and guards are on us; the edge is yours.

How fast is it?

Median 70 ms from a Binance trade to our decision, measured on 16 422 live states, with the bot in AWS Ohio. Our own code adds under 2 ms; an alarm fires if it ever does not.

Which markets?

Kalshi and Polymarket 15-minute and hourly crypto markets first (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, BNB), where our data and backtests live. Other markets on request.
