Do you hold my funds?

No. Your account, your keys. We only place and cancel orders in your name. Revoke any time.

Which markets?

Everything Kalshi and Polymarket list: weather, politics, economics, sports, crypto, culture.

How do you know your latency is the lowest?

We never assume it. For Kalshi we run continuous round-trip probes from inside the exchange’s own cloud region, availability zone by availability zone, to find the edge closest to the matching engine. When the measurement moves, the machines move: we relocate to the nearest placement. Every fill carries its reaction time, so you see it too.

Will it make money?

That is your strategy’s job. Ours: run it fast, within your limits, and show you exactly what it did.
