FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Polybot?

A hosted platform that turns a strategy written in plain English into a trading bot on Kalshi and Polymarket, runs it next to the exchange with hard risk limits, and logs every order with its latency. It is the stack we built and ran with our own money from September to October 2026.

Do you hold my funds?

No. On Kalshi you give an API key scoped to trading; on Polymarket you connect your own wallet. We place and cancel orders in your name. You can revoke access at any time.

Will the bot make money?

That depends on your rule, and Polybot is built to find out fast: backtest on real order books in minutes, paper-trade on live prices, then go live with hard limits. We ran 170 strategies through this exact loop ourselves. Execution, speed and guards are on us; the edge is yours.

How fast is it?

As fast as the venue allows: your bot is co-located in the same cloud region as the exchange’s matching engine, so the round trip is the exchange’s own, not the internet’s. Our code adds under 2 ms, and an alarm fires if it ever does not. For the last microseconds, an institutional fibre market-data line is available on request, billed at cost.

Can I use it from Claude or Codex?

Yes. Polybot ships an MCP server: add one entry to your assistant’s config and it can create, backtest, deploy, pause and kill your bots and read your fills, through seven scoped tools. Your exchange keys stay on Polybot; the assistant never sees them. Available on every plan.

Which markets?

Kalshi and Polymarket 15-minute and hourly crypto markets first (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, BNB), where our data and backtests live. Other markets on request.
